# Recovery Submission Structure

Each repository submission shall contain:

1. **Repository Recovery Tracker** – status of all required batches
2. **Repository Sign-Off** – lead approval
3. **Test Report** – unit, integration, E2E results
4. **API Evidence** – endpoint responses
5. **Screenshots** – UI/dashboard proofs
6. **Logs** – relevant application logs
7. **Metrics Evidence** – Prometheus metrics output
8. **Backup Evidence** – backup/restore proofs (where applicable)
9. **Risk Register** – identified and accepted risks
10. **Blocker Register** – current blockers and status
11. **Blueprint Alignment Evidence** – certification report
12. **Release Gate Evidence** – GO/NO-GO result

Submissions missing any mandatory section shall be returned for completion.
